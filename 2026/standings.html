What is Best Drive?

The best drive of the race, not the fastest. It rewards drivers who improve, race clean and move up, wherever they finish.

How to earn Best Drive

1

Gain places
Every place you gain from the grid to the finish counts. AI excluded.

2

Beat your own Rhythm
Be steadier than in your last five races. You are measured against yourself.

3

Keep it clean
Fewer cuts than the field. A contact the stewards blame on you costs a lot.

4

Race close
Laps spent close behind or alongside another driver count.

5

A midfield bonus
The further you are from the top of the standings, the more your drive counts.

Who decides

The race data names the candidates. Len checks the race video and the stewards have the final say. The race winner cannot win it, and you must finish 75% of the race.

2026 is a trial: we are testing how Best Drive is chosen, so it is an award only, no points. From 2027: +3 points, 6 on a Crown Jewel.
